Family categories advance broadly; EB-2, EB-3 also move forward in November

The November 2026 Visa Bulletin shows broad advancement across all family-sponsored preference categories. F1, F2B, F3, and F4 final action dates moved forward by roughly a year for most countries, while F2A advanced about one month. Employment-based EB-2 and EB-3 final action dates also advanced, and USCIS will use the Dates for Filing chart for both family-sponsored and employment-based applications this month.

  • F1 (All Chargeability Areas, China, India) final action date advanced 366 days, from January 22, 2020 to January 22, 2021; Mexico advanced 14 days to September 22, 2008
  • F2A (All Chargeability Areas) advanced 30 days to October 22, 2026; Mexico advanced 31 days to April 22, 2026
  • F2B advanced 366 days to August 22, 2020; F3 advanced 365 days to October 22, 2015; F4 advanced 366 days to October 22, 2012
  • EB-2 (All Chargeability Areas, Mexico, Philippines) advanced 90 days to April 1, 2025; EB-3 (All Chargeability Areas, Mexico) advanced 31 days to June 15, 2024; EB-1 China advanced 31 days to August 1, 2023
  • USCIS will accept both family-sponsored and employment-based adjustment of status filings this month based on the Dates for Filing chart
